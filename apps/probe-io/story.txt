THE LANTERN ROAD — a test passage for probe-io
“Are you certain?” asked Renée, holding the lantern high.
The café was closed; its façade glowed under the rain…
‘Only one door stays open,’ said the old señor,
“and it is not the one you think.”
Über the bridge, past the Straße of the clockmakers,
the path forks in two:
  — turn to 12 if you follow the river,
  — turn to 47 if you climb towards the château.
Zoë counted her coins: 3 € and a brass token marked «Ø».
Naïve travellers trust the map; wise ones trust the crows.
The signpost reads Århus, Köln, Łódź, Athènes — Αθήνα.
“Keep the lantern lit,” she whispered, “whatever the crows say.”
At the third milestone a voice calls your name. Do you answer?
If you answer, turn to 88. If you keep walking, turn to 104.
The rain thins; somewhere a bell rings eleven, then twelve.
Your pack holds: rope, bread, a copper key, and the map’s torn corner.
